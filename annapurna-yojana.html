Annapurna Yojana 2026 in West Bengal

Information reviewed: 19 November 2026

Annapurna Yojana is a welfare scheme introduced by the Government of West Bengal to provide monthly financial assistance to eligible women residing in the state. The scheme aims to support women's financial security and socio-economic empowerment.

₹3,000 per month

Monthly financial assistance for eligible beneficiaries, subject to government verification and approval.

This guide explains the main benefits, eligibility conditions, required documents and application process based on information published by the Government of West Bengal.

1. What Is Annapurna Yojana?

Annapurna Yojana is a West Bengal Government welfare initiative for eligible women. It provides assured monthly financial assistance of ₹3,000 to support their economic well-being.

The scheme is administered by the Department of Women and Child Development and Social Welfare, Government of West Bengal.

The government notification introducing the scheme is dated 19 May 2026, and the financial assistance is specified to take effect from 1 June 2026.

2. Benefits of Annapurna Yojana

  • Eligible beneficiaries are entitled to ₹3,000 per month.
  • Financial assistance is transferred through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
  • The amount is credited to the beneficiary's Aadhaar-linked bank account.
  • The scheme is intended to improve women's financial security and socio-economic condition.
Important: Receiving the benefit depends on eligibility, verification and approval by the competent government authority.

3. Eligibility Criteria

According to the published scheme information, an applicant must satisfy the applicable conditions, including:

  • The applicant must be a woman residing in West Bengal.
  • The applicant must be between 25 and 60 years of age.
  • The applicant must not be an income tax payer.
  • The applicant must not hold permanent government employment with regular salary or pension under the Central or State Government or the listed government, local and aided institutions.

The Government of West Bengal's scheme information also specifies that the applicant must not be an illegal immigrant. The competent authority will check the applicable conditions during verification.

Please note: Meeting the age and other basic conditions does not automatically guarantee approval. The government authority will verify the application and supporting information.

4. Documents Required

The Government of West Bengal's scheme information lists the following mandatory documents and details:

Document / Detail Purpose
EPIC / Voter ID Card Identity and voter details.
Aadhaar Card Identity verification.
Active Mobile Number Communication and application-related updates.
Aadhaar-linked Bank Account Required for receiving approved financial assistance through DBT.

The official Family Level Data Collection Form may also require family and household information. Check the latest government form before submitting your details.

5. How to Apply for Annapurna Yojana

New applicants can use the official Annapurna Yojana portal or follow the government-directed offline process.

Online Application

  1. Visit the official Annapurna Yojana portal.
  2. Find the available application or registration facility.
  3. Enter the information requested in the official form.
  4. Provide the required identity and family details.
  5. Check the information carefully before submission.
  6. Follow the portal instructions to complete the process.

Use only the official government portal for online application and status-related services.

Visit Official Annapurna Portal

Offline Application

The Government of West Bengal also describes an offline submission facility for the Family Level Data Collection Form.

  • Rural applicants can approach the Block Development Officer (BDO) office.
  • Urban applicants can approach the Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO) office.
  • Applicants within the Kolkata Municipal Corporation area can approach the relevant Ward office.

6. Application Verification and Approval

Applications are subject to verification and enquiry by authorised government officials.

  • In rural areas, the BDO handles the relevant verification and recommendation process.
  • In urban areas, the SDO handles the relevant verification and recommendation process.
  • In the Kolkata Municipal Corporation area, the designated KMC officials handle the process.
  • The District Magistrate is the sanctioning authority for districts.
  • The Commissioner of Kolkata Municipal Corporation is the sanctioning authority within the KMC area.

Applicants should follow the official instructions and contact their concerned office if additional verification or information is required.

9. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How much financial assistance is provided?

The scheme provides ₹3,000 per month to eligible beneficiaries, subject to government verification and approval.

What is the eligible age limit?

The published scheme conditions specify an age range of 25 to 60 years.

Can an income tax payer apply?

No. The published eligibility conditions exclude income tax payers.

Can a permanent government employee apply?

The scheme conditions exclude the specified permanent government employees receiving regular salary or pension.

Where can I apply?

New applicants can use the official online portal. The government also describes submission through BDO, SDO and relevant KMC Ward offices.

Which documents are listed by the government?

The Government of West Bengal lists EPIC (Voter ID), Aadhaar Card and an active mobile number. An Aadhaar-linked bank account is needed for DBT.

Is this an official government website?

No. West Bengal Government Schemes A to Z is an independent public information website. Applications must be made through the official government channels.
