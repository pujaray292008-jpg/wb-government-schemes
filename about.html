About Us

Welcome to West Bengal Government Schemes A to Z. We are an independent information website created to help people find information about government schemes, public services and useful online resources.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make information about government schemes and public services easier to understand and access. We aim to provide simple guides, useful details and links to relevant official websites.

What We Provide

Our website provides general information about government schemes, eligibility requirements, application guidance and links to official portals. We also share information about public services, scholarships and other useful resources.

Our Commitment

We aim to keep our content clear, helpful and easy to understand. Visitors should always verify the latest information, eligibility rules and application procedures through the relevant official government website.

Independent Information Website

Important Notice: West Bengal Government Schemes A to Z is an independent informational website. It is not affiliated with, authorised by or operated by any government department or agency.

We do not process government applications, issue certificates or provide official government services. All applications and services must be accessed through the respective official portals.
